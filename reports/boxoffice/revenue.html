Sell-through vs. the 1,200-seat house

Lever 1 — rescue the soft night (+$8,300). Glass Anthem left 660 seats empty at 45% sell-through. Getting the weakest booking to just 65% — still below every other night this season — adds ~240 tickets at ~$35 average net.

How to act on it: book the weak slot against demand signals (the top-grossing genres out-drew it 2× at higher prices), and give the soft night the early-window push — the pacing chart below shows the launch window is where tickets actually sell.

Tier mix: VIP is capped, not weak

Lever 2 — reprice / expand VIP (+$4,900). VIP is the smallest bar on every row below, but that's a supply ceiling, not soft demand: VIP's 100 seats sold out on 6 of 8 nights. When a tier sells out almost every night, it is underpriced. A 10% VIP price increase on sellout-calibre nights ($48.7K of VIP net) yields the estimate with no extra cost; adding 20 VIP seats where the room allows is worth roughly the same again.

Sales pacing: the launch window does the heavy lifting

Daily net revenue across the on-sale window. The first four days sold $116.8K — 35% of the season — before settling into a trough. The lesson for next season: invest in the on-sale moment and the announce, and rescue soft nights early, while the buying window is still long.
