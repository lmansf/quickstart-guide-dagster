Sell-through vs. the 1,200-seat house

Lever 1 — rescue the soft night (+$8,400). Glass Anthem left 660 seats empty; every other night sold at least 74%. Getting the weakest booking to just 65% sell-through — still below this season's second-worst night — adds ~240 tickets at ~$35 average net.

Tier mix: VIP is capped, not weak

Lever 2 — reprice / expand VIP (+$4,500). VIP is the smallest bar on every row below, but that's a supply ceiling, not soft demand: VIP's 100 seats sold out on 6 of 8 nights. When a tier sells out almost every night, it is underpriced. A 10% VIP price increase on sellout-calibre nights (~$44.7K of VIP net) yields ~$4,500 with no extra cost; adding 20 VIP seats where the room allows is worth roughly the same again.

Sales pacing: the launch window does the heavy lifting

Daily net revenue across the 18-day on-sale window. The first four days sold $116.8K — 35% of the season — before settling into a ~$16K/day trough. The lesson for next season: invest in the on-sale moment and the announce, and rescue soft nights early, while the buying window is still long.
