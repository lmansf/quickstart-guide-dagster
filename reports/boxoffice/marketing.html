First, fix the measurement: $13,703 was credited to nobody

150 orders carried mistyped promo codes (' SUMMER25', 'vipnight', …) and matched no campaign, so the as-recorded leaderboard crowned Radio Week best-by-revenue. Normalizing the codes (trim + uppercase in stg_orders — the pipeline's README Step 5) reassigns the missing revenue and changes the answer: Summer Kickoff leads on revenue and VIP Love Letter leads on efficiency. Also invisible to code attribution: Retarget Blitz ($950, no code at all). Next season, every campaign ships with a canonical code, and order forms normalize codes at entry — otherwise the budget reallocation below optimizes against noise.

Attendance: sold is not the same as showed up

1,182 sold tickets never crossed a gate. Ticket revenue is already banked, but empty seats forfeit bar and concession spend and erode repeat business. Static Bloom is the outlier — 12 pts below the house average of 83.6%.

Upside not counted in the +5% bridge: the sold-out night still had empty seats from no-shows. A day-of waitlist that re-releases confirmed no-shows at face value monetizes the same seat twice on sellout nights.

The action plan on this page

  1. Lever 3 — reallocate ~$2,000 of spend ≈ +$7,100 net

    Cut Last Call Push ($1,100 at $7.34/$ — it ran after the buying window had mostly closed) and trim the next-worst channel. Redeploy into scaled-up versions of the top performers led by VIP Love Letter ($29.28/$), aimed at the launch window and at soft nights. Sized as $2,000 moved from $9.45/$ channels to $23.65/$ channels, discounted to 25% incrementality.

  2. Lever 4 — exchange-first refund policy ≈ +$3,800 net

    $25,637 (7.1% of gross) left as cash refunds. Default the refund flow to a swap-night or venue credit with a small fee for cash-out; converting 15% of leakage to retained revenue clears the estimate. Bonus: credits pull people back into the building, where concessions margin lives.

  3. Prerequisite — trustworthy attribution $13,703 reassigned

    Normalize promo codes in the pipeline (README Step 5), validate codes at checkout, and give every paid campaign a canonical code — including Retarget Blitz, whose $950 is currently unmeasurable. This page already computes attribution on cleaned codes; the staging fix makes the rest of the pipeline agree.

  4. Watch-item — Static Bloom's no-shows

    254 no-shows on one night. Send day-of reminders with door-time and transit info for late-week shows, and pilot the sellout-night waitlist re-release on the next sellout-calibre event.
