First, fix the measurement: $13,703 was credited to nobody

150 orders carried mistyped promo codes (' SUMMER25', 'vipnight', …) and matched no campaign, so the season's official report crowned Radio Week best-by-revenue and missed VIP Love Letter entirely. Normalizing the codes (trim + uppercase in stg_orders — the pipeline's README Step 5) reassigns all $13,703 and changes the answer: Summer Kickoff leads on revenue and VIP Love Letter leads on efficiency. Attendance: sold is not the same as showed up

1,182 sold tickets never crossed a gate (16.4% of kept tickets). Ticket revenue is already banked, but empty seats forfeit bar and concession spend and erode repeat business. Static Bloom is the outlier — 12 points below the house average of 83.6%.

The action plan on this page

  1. Lever 3 — reallocate ~$2K of spend ≈ +$4,000 net

    Cut Last Call Push ($1,100 at $7.34/$ — it ran after the buying window had mostly closed) and trim Genre Nights by ~$400 ($12.17/$). Redeploy into scaled-up versions of VIP Love Letter, Encore Newsletter, and Student Rush ($22–29/$), aimed at the launch window and at soft nights. Sized with a 25% incrementality haircut on the attributed delta.

  2. Lever 4 — exchange-first refund policy ≈ +$3,800 net

    $25,637 (7.1% of gross) left as cash refunds. Default the refund flow to a swap-night or venue credit with a small fee for cash-out; converting 15% of leakage to retained revenue clears the estimate. Bonus: credits pull people back into the building, where concessions margin lives.

  3. Prerequisite — trustworthy attribution $13,703 reassigned

    Normalize promo codes in the pipeline (README Step 5), validate codes at checkout, and give every paid campaign a canonical code — including Retarget Blitz, whose $950 is currently unmeasurable. Re-run this report after the fix lands; the budget moves above depend on these numbers.

  4. Watch-item — Static Bloom's no-shows

    254 no-shows (28.6%) on one night. Send day-of reminders with door-time and transit info for late-week shows, and pilot the sellout-night waitlist re-release on the next Neon Coyote-calibre event.
