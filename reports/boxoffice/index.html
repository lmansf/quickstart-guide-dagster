Three findings that drive the plan

Demand is there — supply is misallocated

The best night (Neon Coyote, $56.3K net) out-earned the worst (Glass Anthem, $18.7K at 45% of the house) by 3.0×. VIP hit its cap 6 of 8 nights. The best-to-worst spread alone is 2.2× the entire gap to target.

Marketing dollars vary 4× in what they return

The best campaign returned $29.28 of net revenue per dollar (VIP Love Letter, $120 spend); the worst tracked returned $7.34 (Last Call Push, $1,100 spend) — and $950 of facebook spend is invisible to attribution entirely.

$13,703 of revenue was credited to nobody
