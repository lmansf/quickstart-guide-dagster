Three findings that drive the plan

Demand is there — supply is misallocated

One night sold out completely (Neon Coyote, $56.3K net) while another sold 45% of the house (Glass Anthem, $18.7K). VIP hit its cap 6 nights of 8; GA hit its cap once. The spread between best and worst night is 3× the entire gap to target.

Marketing dollars vary 4× in what they return

The best campaign returned $29.28 of net revenue per dollar (VIP Love Letter, $120 spend); the worst returned $7.34 (Last Call Push, $1,100 spend) — and $950 of Facebook spend is invisible to attribution entirely.

$13,703 of revenue was credited to nobody

150 orders carried mistyped promo codes and joined no campaign. Cleaning the codes re-crowns the leaderboard — the "best campaign" changes — so this fix comes first, before any budget is moved.
